Consumer apps by product

US iPhone only. Sorted by new ratings in the last 7 days, which shows what is happening now. Total ratings favor old apps. Apple does not publish download counts, so new ratings and chart rank are the free stand-ins for downloads. Muse from Meta is a newer Meta app (released September 2026) and ranks far above the older Meta AI app. A new app shows its 7-day gain after 7 days of history.

Rank is on the US App Store top-free chart (top 200) and the Productivity chart. Reviews per day uses the newest 500 written reviews. Apple\'s review feed is unreliable: on some days it returns no reviews, so that column shows the newest good reading with its date. The 7-day ratings gain needs a snapshot from a week ago.

Web traffic rank

How popular each lab’s chat site is on the open web, from Cloudflare Radar. It ranks sites by how many people look them up, so it covers the web, not apps. Rank 1 is the top.
